Nam Knobs is installed

  1. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins if it doesn't pick up Nam Knobs by itself (it is listed under VFX Chris).
  2. Logic Pro: the Audio Unit is checked automatically the next time Logic starts.
  3. Drag a folder of .nam captures onto the Nam Knobs window to build your first amp.

To remove Nam Knobs later, run Uninstall Nam Knobs from the disk image.
