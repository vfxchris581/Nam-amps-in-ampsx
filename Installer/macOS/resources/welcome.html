Welcome to Nam Knobs __VERSION__

This installer puts Nam Knobs where your music software looks for it:

  • VST3 plug-in → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit (Logic, GarageBand, MainStage) → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • Standalone app → /Applications

You can untick any of them on the Installation Type page with Customize.

Quit your DAW before continuing. Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later.

Your amps, blends and presets are kept. An existing Nam Knobs install is replaced with this version.
